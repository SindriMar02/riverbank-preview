The house that once kept the town’s accounts now welcomes guests on the banks of the River Rangá.

Five newly refurbished apartments in the old bank building, every one of them fully equipped, with free parking at the door. Riverbank is run by Örvar and Harpa, who answer the phone themselves.

One house, five fully equipped apartments

Hella has everything you need to make Riverbank your base. The grocery store is about a hundred metres from the door, the bakery about the same, and the swimming pool with its geothermal hot tubs is a few minutes’ walk. Measured, not estimated.
