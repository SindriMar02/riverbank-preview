Riverbank · October to April

The dark half of the year

The photograph above was taken from this building. Hella sits 94 kilometres east of Reykjavík, off the edge of the city’s light, and from October to April the nights here are long enough to wait one out from your own doorstep.

How dark it actually gets

Computed for Þrúðvangur 5

4h 20mDaylight, 21 December
13hTrue darkness that night
11:11Sunrise at midwinter
15:29Sunset at midwinter

On the shortest day the sun is up for 4 hours 20 minutes and there are around 13 hours when it sits more than eighteen degrees below the horizon, which is the darkness the aurora needs. By mid November it is 6 hours 40 minutes of daylight, and by mid February 8 hours 40 minutes. Those are computed for this address, not for Reykjavík and not for Iceland in general.

About the northern lights

We will not promise them. They need a clear sky and an active night, and both are weather rather than something a booking can include. What the house can offer is the part that is actually in its gift: a dark enough sky, a door you can step out of at two in the morning without driving anywhere, and somewhere warm with a kitchen to come back into.

  • Step outside, not drive outFree parking and the garden are at the door, so waiting out a cloudy hour costs nothing.
  • A kitchen of your ownEvery apartment is self-catering with its own kitchen and dishwasher, which matters more in January than in July.
  • A washing machine in every apartmentWet gear dries overnight instead of travelling with you.
  • The pool at 165 metresHella’s swimming pool and its geothermal hot tubs are a few minutes’ walk from the door.

What guests ask before a winter stay

Örvar and Harpa, who answer the phone

Can you see the northern lights from Riverbank?

Yes. The aurora photograph on the Riverbank winter page was taken from the building itself. Nobody can promise them, though: they need a clear sky and an active night, and both are weather, not a booking option.

How dark does it get in Hella in winter?

On 21 December the sun rises at 11:11 and sets at 15:29, which is about 4 hours 20 minutes of daylight and roughly 13 hours a night of true darkness. By mid November it is 6 hours 40 minutes of daylight, and by mid February 8 hours 40 minutes.

Is Riverbank open in winter?

Yes. Guests have reviewed stays in all twelve months, and 32 of the 177 published reviews are from stays between October and April.

How far is Riverbank from Reykjavík and the airport?

Reykjavík is 94 kilometres by road and Keflavík International Airport is 135 kilometres, both on Route 1. Allow well over the free-flow driving times in winter conditions.

Is there a shop nearby, and somewhere to swim?

The grocery store is about 100 metres from the door, the bakery about the same, and Hella swimming pool with its geothermal hot tubs is about 165 metres away. Parking is free at the door.

Do the apartments have kitchens?

Every apartment is self-catering with its own private kitchen and a dishwasher, and every one has a washing machine. In the low season, when fewer places are serving food late, that is the difference between cooking and driving.

Are pets allowed?

No. Riverbank has a no-pets policy at present.
