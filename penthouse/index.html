What guests ask about the Penthouse

How many people can sleep in the Penthouse?

Eight, in four bedrooms: one with an extra-large double bed, two with large doubles, and one with two single beds. There are two bathrooms, and 160 square metres in all.

Is there a proper kitchen?

Yes. The Penthouse is self-catering, with an open kitchen around a marble island, a dishwasher and a coffee machine. Every apartment in the house has a kitchen of its own.

Is there a washing machine?

Yes, a washer and dryer in one.

What can you see from the balcony?

The garden, and the river Rangá beyond it. The photograph captioned “From the balcony of the penthouse” was taken standing on it.

Is parking included?

Yes, free parking at the door, and there is a fast charger for electric cars directly across the street. The grocery store is about 100 metres away, so the car can stay where it is.

Are pets allowed?

No. Riverbank has a no-pets policy at present.
