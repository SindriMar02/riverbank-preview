The Penthouse

160 square metres, four bedrooms, two bathrooms, beds for eight, and a balcony looking over the garden to the Rangá. The largest home in the old bank house, kept by one family at a time.

Charcoal, rust and stone

Newly refurbished from end to end: an open kitchen with a marble island, a living room under dark timber with a 75-inch screen, and a stairwell painted the colour of the river’s autumn banks. Air conditioning, a dishwasher, a washer and dryer in one, and streaming on the TV are all in.
