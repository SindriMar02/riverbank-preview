What guests ask about the four

How many people fit in a one-bedroom apartment?

Four. Each has a large double bed in the bedroom and a sofa bed in the living room, and one of the four has an extra day bed as well.

Does each apartment have its own kitchen and bathroom?

Yes. All four are complete apartments of 55 square metres, each with a fully equipped kitchen, a washer/dryer and a bathroom of its own.

What is the difference between the four?

The beds and the view. One has an extra day bed as well as the sofa bed, and one looks onto the garden. All four are the same 55 square metres, with the same fully equipped kitchen and washer/dryer.

Can a larger group take more than one?

Yes. Each apartment is let separately and the four are set side by side, so a larger group can book two together and comfortably sleep up to eight guests.

Is parking included?

Yes, free parking at the door, and there is a fast charger for electric cars directly across the street. The grocery store is about 100 metres away, so the car can stay where it is.

Are pets allowed?

No. Riverbank has a no-pets policy at present.
