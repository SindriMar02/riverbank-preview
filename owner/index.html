What a direct booking keeps

Your numbers, measured

The defaults are your own figures, not guesses. 70.000 kr is the season-weighted average of your live Booking.com prices, read on 31 August 2026. 30 platform nights a month is the occupancy measured on your own calendar over the same period. Change any of them and the arithmetic follows.

Booking.com’s base rate for independent properties in Europe is 15%. Airbnb’s host-only fee is 15.5%, but only for hosts on property-management software; on the split fee it is 3%. Yours may differ.
